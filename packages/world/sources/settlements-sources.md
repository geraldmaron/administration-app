# Settlement context sources

Inspected 2026-10-14. Rebuild offline with `node scripts/bake-cities.mjs`.

- Natural Earth, Populated Places 5.1.2: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-populated-places/ . The existing vendored `scripts/vendor/ne-us-places.tsv` supplies names, state attribution, coordinates and population estimates. The publisher describes sampled city/town points and urban-agglomeration estimates, not a complete municipal census. “Small” (up to 100,000 in this source) is a model tier; it proves neither legal town status nor rural character, crops, a particular industry or a municipal service capacity. No employment/tax/output figures are generated for this table.
- Port Authority of NY/NJ, Port Newark/Elizabeth marine-terminal map: https://www.panynj.gov/content/dam/port/doing-business-pdfs/port-newark-elizabeth-marine-terminal.pdf . The opened map identifies both terminals and Port Newark. Newark's point/state/population come from the same vendored Natural Earth row; the commercial-port flag comes from this terminal evidence. This makes a New Jersey commercial-coast scene eligible for Newark rather than using Atlantic City as the only local candidate. It does not assert a naval shipyard, military production or a specific cargo capacity.

The larger metro/economic city table remains unchanged. Settlement points are geographic context, so they do not alter national population, founding person draws or aggregate economic accounts.

- GSA, International Falls Land Port of Entry: https://www.gsa.gov/real-estate/ongoing-construction-projects/international-falls-land-port-of-entry . Opened the project page and its description locating the land port at Highway 53/International Bridge opposite Fort Frances. This supports the road-border flag for the vendored International Falls point. A new Minnesota road-border scene uses this evidenced locality rather than treating Duluth as a road crossing. The point remains an area dateline; no inspection throughput, current wait, funding obligation or future construction completion is inferred. The CBP/DHS PDF endpoints returned 403; the public GSA page supplied the opened primary evidence instead.
