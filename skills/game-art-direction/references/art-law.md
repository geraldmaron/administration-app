# Generated-asset review criteria

These criteria apply to generated raster assets. They do not prohibit expressive UI graphics, icon badges, maps, scene-specific color, diagrams, gradients or textures. Current interface direction lives in `docs/DESIGN.md`.

## Match the role

A dispatch scene uses documentary framing appropriate to its scenario, site and condition. An ambient asset establishes setting with a composition that fits its surface. Choose natural color and scene-specific contrast rather than imposing a universal muted grade. A new illustration style needs a coherent use case; it need not imitate a document or photograph unless the asset's role calls for that.

The existing scene pipeline produces photographic assets. Its photographic negatives apply to that pipeline, not to all future art classes. Portraits are record-driven, so this skill contains no portrait-generation prompt.

## Preserve story truth

Reusable scenes must not identify a country through an incompatible landmark, flag or inscription. Do not depict damage, casualties or activity beyond the scenario condition. Never use illustrative assets as measured evidence, intelligence or an exact forecast. Real geography and country flags can still be shown through the application's corresponding data-backed components.

## Review at the destination

Check recognizable living-person likeness, readable generated text, watermarks, real logos and insignia; reject these in generated scene assets. Check coherence, crop, scene fit and actual display sizes. Preserve visual detail outside any necessary text-safe zone. Atmospheric layers must not obscure controls or essential information.

Keep provenance and byte/format validation. Reject false content and broken assets, not an asset merely for being vivid, illustrated or more visually expressive than an old reference.
