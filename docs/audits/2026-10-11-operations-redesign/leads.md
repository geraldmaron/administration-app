### Duplicate-submit guard
Two layers. The component layer is a `useRef` flag plus `busy` on the button (above). The real guard is the store: `commit` publishes synchronously before it awaits the save, so a second press holding the same `basis` fails the identity check (`store.ts:386-393`). That is why the two hooks without a ref (GodModeRow, MatterRow) are still safe. A press on a different surface in the same tick is not blocked at the component layer, but its own `basis` is stale after the first publish.

### Stale-price detection
`store.ts:106-116`: `basis !== state.campaign` (object identity) throws `STALE_ACT_MESSAGE` ("The situation changed after this was prepared. Nothing was applied"). Consequences:
- Any publish refuses a held draft, including unrelated ones (a save-state change does not publish the campaign, but any act or time step does).
- The text the player sees is not that sentence. `shell/advance.ts:25` maps it to "Time had already moved on. Look at the Brief before you continue." That is wrong for a stale press caused by another act, and it names time. Used by all hooks above.
- No diff is shown ("what changed"), and nothing stale is re-priced on demand: the screen re-prices because `campaign` is a prop and the assessment is a `useMemo` over it (`Decision.tsx:79-81`, `BudgetScreen.tsx:62`), so the refreshed numbers appear, but the player gets no statement of which terms moved. Brief 9.3 asks for that statement.

### Save failure
Correct and consistent in the receipts: "It stands, but this device did not save it: ..." in `decision/DecisionReceipt.tsx:27-28`, `nation/policy/ActDone.tsx:30`, `nation/economy/ActReceipt.tsx:32-33`, `washington/executive/ActReceipt.tsx:28`, `world/nation/ActReceipt.tsx:69`, `nation/state/LocaleReceipt.tsx:43`, `brief/CaseScreen.tsx:63`. The act is not rolled back (`store.ts:84-91` explains why). The top bar shows Retry save with a door to recovery (`shell/SaveStatus.tsx:36-52`); `retrySave` rewrites the current campaign and does not re-run the act. Gaps: ScandalScreen shows no save error (`:101`); MatterRow drops its receipt (`:51`); GodModeRow says only "this device did not save it" (`:43`); and in `decision/acts.ts:45` and `washington/executive/acts.ts:46` a non-ready store after commit makes the hook return with no message and no receipt.

### Post-commit navigation default
Stay in context. Each hook holds the receipt in component state and the screen swaps the desk for the receipt in place (`nation/policy/OrderScreen.tsx:153-155`, `decision/Decision.tsx:136-139`, `nation/budget/Request.tsx:73-80`). "Open the Record entry" is an explicit door (`ActDone.tsx:32-36`). No hook navigates. Mandatory transitions after a commit (campaign ending, recovery, changed administration) are not checked in any receipt component; the top bar swaps Continue for an ended notice (`ContinueControl.tsx:52-54`) but the receipt stays up (unverified in a browser).

Back-button and reload: the receipt is local React state, so reload or Back-then-Forward brings the desk back, priced on the current campaign. For decisions that is safe, because the screen resolves the cast from the live campaign and shows "This decision is not waiting" once answered (`Decision.tsx:118-133,138-140`; `castStillBound` check at `decision/acts.ts:77`). The player then loses the receipt: it exists only on the Record. For other acts (orders, tariffs) whether a signed act can show its desk again was not verified: `signOrder` rejects unavailable orders (`packages/engine/src/orders.ts:584`), but "already signed" availability was not read. `ActDone`'s "Back to the order" dismisses the receipt and returns to the live desk (`OrderScreen.tsx:154`).

### Draft persistence
All drafts are component state with no storage: the budget draft is `useState` (`nation/budget/BudgetScreen.tsx:60`), the selected compare column is `useState` (`Request.tsx:71`, `Decision.tsx:82`), the tariff and force terms live in the act config in screen state. Only the founding draft has its own module (`founding/draft.ts`), also in memory (no `localStorage` or `sessionStorage` anywhere in `apps/web/src` or `player-core/src`; IndexedDB is used for device settings, `app/device-storage.ts`). Results against brief 9.3:
- No leak between campaigns through storage: nothing is stored. Leakage in memory is guarded: the delta lens reads a module-level `LastChange` (`app/last-change.ts:20-42`) only while `change.after === campaign` (`freshChange`, `:40-42`); it keeps the old campaigns alive until the next change.
- Retention on navigation, reload, withdrawal and campaign switch: not met. Leaving a screen discards the draft; the brief asks to keep it by campaign, subject and action type.
- After success the draft is cleared where it matters (`Request.tsx:74-78` resets selection and calls `onSent`), but the budget draft survives a failed commit and is re-priced against the new campaign.
