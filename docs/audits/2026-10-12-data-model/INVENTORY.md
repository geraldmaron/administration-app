# Data model inventory (partial), 2026-10-12

Read-only mapping of the simulated state, written to seed the metric registry. Partial: the Campaign root, nation/person/state/city/cohort schemas, the consumer registry, the phase sequence, opinion, removal, macro, vitals, the impact model, war and strike paths and scenario storage were traced. Congress, courts, budget/funding, commodities/markets, munitions, intelligence, press, cohorts in depth and flags were located but not traced and are marked `untraced`. Paths are relative to the repository root.

## Summary

1. The live state root is `Campaign` (`packages/engine/src/state-campaign.ts:358-753`), about 80 top-level stores.
2. Player metrics are `MetricSet` (`:147-186`): approval, treasury, stability, militaryReadiness, standing. Public opinion is `PublicMood` (`:195-208`): six drivers plus polarization. Approval is never written by an effect; it is recomputed from the mood (`opinion.ts:79`, `:421`, `:450`).
3. Removal pressure is recomputed every turn from six weighted terms (`removal.ts:77-84`); beats open at 0.45, 0.52 and 0.60 (`:209`).
4. `macroDriftPhase` (`macro.ts:18-139`) is the one writer of the economy's rates: growth, inflation, unemployment, crime and cost of living revert 4% a week toward a target built from several drivers; political figures revert 1% a week.
5. Population is simulated: `vitalsPhase` applies births, deaths and net migration weekly (`vitals.ts:29-36`, `:123-161`); impact events (disasters, outbreaks, strikes, cyberattacks) subtract their deaths (`impact-lifecycle.ts:41-45`). War casualties never reduce population.
6. Casualty defect confirmed: `engagedPersonnel` is half the principal's whole active force (`war-attrition.ts:17`, `:62-66`) times 2% a week (`:26`, `:73-75`), not scaled by theater, committed forces or the opponent.
7. Birth, death and migration rates are fixed for the campaign; no engine code writes them. The consumer registry names `macro.deathBaseline` and `macro.birthBaseline` as readers, but neither exists, so `medianAge` and `literacy` have no simulation reader.
8. The `populationToll` effect kind is defined (`effect-types.ts:137-139`) and applied (`effects.ts:584`) but no engine code emits it; whether content can (`packages/content/src/impact-model.ts`) is untraced.
9. There is no generic delayed-effect queue; timing lives in specific stores (issued acts, dated external shocks, condition spans, daily impact-event stepping, pending rules, per-domain phase clocks).
10. Scenarios: 322 in 18 JSONL shards under `content/corpus/`, many per file, baked byte-for-byte into player-core and parsed lazily on first draw.

## Specific answers

**Population.** Simulated. Births, deaths and migration apply weekly (`vitals.ts:123-161`) at rates fixed for the campaign. Disaster, disease, strike and cyber deaths go through impact events stepped daily in `impact-calculation.ts` (outbreak `:19-36`, physical hazards `:53-79`, cyber `:38-51`) and debited from the nation and US states in `impact-lifecycle.ts:31-46`. War deaths are not debited. Outbreaks track susceptible, infected and recovered people; there is no standing morbidity stock. City populations are fixed.

**Strikes and combat.** `strike-mission.ts:11-28` builds an impact event with a fixed exposure of 1,200 or 2,400 people. Civilian risk (`strike-outcomes.ts:15-27`) is `.08 + urbanization*.2 + (1-intelCapability)*.15 + (1-readiness*doctrine)*.12`, times force scale and a mission factor (1, 1.15 or 1.35), clamped to 0.03-0.65; assessment multiplies it by 0.65. Harm (`:45-80`) is one impact-event step: casualtyRate .08, injuryRatio 3, intensity `.65*forceScale`, exposure from an assessed site profile (80-300 people, `strike-assessment.ts:28-34`) or `100 + 900*urbanization`. Munitions and target capability do not enter; target type enters only through site exposure. War strength (`war-tick.ts:44-53`) is projected force times theater scale times munition factor, ratio at `:121`, swing ±0.3 at `:380`, command quality at `:89-96`. The casualty formula (`war-attrition.ts:17,26,62-75`) is the same weekly scale in any war.

**Timing.** Immediate: effect packets through `apply` (`effects.ts`), with approval recomputed per packet. Spread over time: impact events stepped daily (`impact-lifecycle.ts:77-100`), mean reversion in macro and opinion, dated `externalShocks` (`state-campaign.ts:670`), `issuedActs` (`:575`, `action-timing.ts`), `pendingRules` (`:482`), condition spans, war weeks. Conditional: gates in the 64-phase sequence (`turn-phases.ts:98-295`), condition onset, scenario gates, the `delayed-consequence` case family (`case-families.ts:35`). Trait-modulated: minister competence and loyalty (`war-tick.ts:95`), person attributes (`stat-consumers.ts:310-366`), nation traits feeding NPC agency (`:252-262`). No generic scheduled-effect queue.

**Scenarios.** `content/corpus/shards/*.jsonl` (18 files, one per family) plus `index.jsonl`, `flags.jsonl`, `phrases.jsonl`, `overlap.jsonl` and `manifest.json` (322 scenarios). Baked byte-for-byte into `packages/player-core/src/content/bundled-corpus.generated.ts` with a sha256 per shard; parsed lazily by `bundled-corpus.ts:24-39` through `shard-parser.ts`. Validated by the `ScenarioDef` schema, `corpus build` and a test refusing unparseable lines. Generation workspace: `content/jobs/`; reviews in `content/reviews/<job>/`. File sizes and the id scheme were not checked.

## What a registry gate can derive, and what needs authoring

Derivable: writers from Effect-kind dispatch (`effects.ts:584`) and `setStats` paths in `nationStat` effects; readers from field-path search and selector/import graphs; phase cadence from `campaignPhaseSequence`; clamps from zod `.min`/`.max` and named constants (`MACRO_BOUNDS`); status from declared readers that do not exist (as with `deathBaseline`).

Authored: formula summaries, units and scale beyond `units.ts`, the kind, timing semantics and the intent of decay, whether a metric saturates (needs harness runs), and whether a reader is real. The registry's prose `readBy` strings have already drifted from code at `stat-consumers.ts:55,61,65,71`.
