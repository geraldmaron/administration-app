# Scenario fidelity and the studio, 2026-10-19

Trigger: the owner said the studio is messy and hard to use for managing scenarios, runs, tuning and weights. They want each decision's metric impact visible. They want a scenario's stated act, such as an N% tariff on a good against a country, applied through the metric system. They want scenarios with depth and their needed context, in plain words, believable, and true to the game state as it changes.

Method: read by this session against source at `213b75c37` and the running studio (`apps/studio`, port 4400). The corpus was 330 scenarios and 1,000 replies, counted with a script over `content/corpus/shards/*.jsonl`. This session changed no code. It extends `ASSESSMENT.md` in this folder and does not restate it.

## 1. A scenario's trade act and the engine disagree

Replies reach the trade system in three ways, and none of them can say "lift", "pause" or "sanction":

| Route | What it does | Where |
|---|---|---|
| `commitment.tariff {sector, rate}` | A real tariff through `imposeTariff`, but only against a foreign retaliation the cast pinned | `packages/engine/src/conversation-report-actions.ts:60-75`, `packages/content/src/scenario.ts:448` |
| `instrument: economic, rung 2` | A fixed 10% tariff on the tariffable goods the U.S. imports most from that partner, whatever the prose names | `packages/engine/src/instruments-economic.ts:82`, `:84-87`, `:113-125` |
| `position` with `cost` only | Stability, standing or treasury deltas; no trade measure changes | — |

The engine already has `liftMeasure`, but no reply can reach it. No audit compares the act in a reply's prose with what the reply's commitment does. The only unrouted-act rule is `unrouted-domestic-act`.

Replies whose prose acts on trade but whose effect is only a generic delta:

- `the-draft-from-the-crossing-talks/sign-the-draft-now`: "announce the thirty-day pause on our tariff". The tariff stays in force.
- `the-offer-to-lift-both-duties-together/take-the-same-morning`: both duties end. Neither measure is lifted.
- `the-trade-terms-our-neighbor-wants/take-the-deal`: a grain tariff cut books `treasury +30`. Cutting a tariff lowers customs revenue, so the sign is backwards. The rejoinder's "grain cheaper in the cities by a tenth" is not believable for a country that exports grain.
- `six-at-the-checkpoint/condemn-and-sanction`: "announce sectoral sanctions". No sanction measure is created.
- `the-toll-on-the-narrow-road/match-the-toll` and `the-tariff-that-answered-a-tariff/mirror-the-tariff`: "mirror their rate". Rung 2 posts 10% on the goods bought most from them.

**Correction, same day.** The lift and pause replies were not careless. The doctrine then in force (`CONTENT_DOCTRINE.md` §10, amended 2026-10-17) forbade a reply from lifting, so their rejoinders said the lift order still awaited the President's signature on the Trade screen. The defect was the rule and the missing route, not the writing. BL-268 retires that rule (§10 as amended 2026-10-19).

## 2. Scenarios that state a trade fact the ledger may not hold

These scenarios state a tariff that nothing in the campaign establishes:

- `the-trade-terms-our-neighbor-wants`: the particulars say "Our tariff on grain". Its only gate is `counterpart.trust ≥ 0.2`, and its `universal` premise reason covers the offer, not the tariff.
- `the-tariff-that-answered-a-tariff`: titled "Their tariff that answered ours", gated only on a relationship. Its `introduces` premise creates their tariff in the prose but writes no measure, and nothing establishes ours.
- `the-factory-shutdown-spreads` and `the-counterpart-is-gone` refer to tariff schedules with no trade gate.

The newer trade family (`cast: tradeMeasure`, `tariffInForce`) does bind real measures. Its lift and pause replies are the cost-only ones above.

These scenarios closed BL-245's premise debt by declaring a premise. A declared premise satisfies the audit but does not make the trade ledger agree with the prose. The player's trade screen can show no grain tariff while the memo argues about one.

## 3. The studio

Observed in the running studio:

- **Impacts are not visible.** The Library's decision tree prints the authored `cost` ("treasury +30, stability +0.04") and the reply's flags. It does not print what the engine would apply: resolved impact bands, an instrument rung's effects, a tariff's measure and customs line, or treasury in dollars. An instrument reply shows nothing about its effect.
- **The preview is wrong.** "Play it" offers the United States as its own counterpart ("United States wants tariff concessions on grain imports"). The page title shows a raw `{{counterpart}}`.
- **Runs hides the current program.** 78 jobs "the console cannot render", every `scenario-repair` job of the alignment program among them. `apps/studio/job-kinds.mjs` knows four kinds; `studio-cli/src/scenarios/upgrade.ts:12` defines seven revision kinds. The header counts 1,352 items open and 392 failed with no age or status breakdown.
- **Counts disagree without saying why.** Explorer says 368 scenarios; the shipped corpus has 330. Metrics lists 165 dataset fields and says "no registered reader" for `sectors`, a field its own description says is read. `scripts/metric-registry-report.json` covers 284 fields.
- **No tuning or weights surface.** Nothing in `apps/studio` reads `OPINION_WEIGHTS`, `PRESSURE_WEIGHTS`, `BEAT_THRESHOLDS`, the cabinet constants, `IMPACT_MODEL_VERSION`, or scenario `weight` and `boosts` as a set. Balance-harness runs appear nowhere.
- **Navigation is split.** "Scenarios" (`/explore`) and "Library" (`/library`) both browse scenarios. "Runs" lives at `/scenarios`. The rail groups pages by data type, not by the task they serve.

## 4. Depth, context and plain words

Every shipped scenario carries `context.standing`, `precedent`, `pressures`, `inaction` and `unknown` and particulars. The opening median is 105 words, the minimum 49. Structure is not the gap. Truth against the board is the gap (sections 1 and 2, `ASSESSMENT.md` §1 and §2), along with BL-226's 125 replies that promise powers a President lacks. No content-review check asks whether a reply's prose matches its mechanical effect.
