# Domestic force acts: risk resolution plan

Status: plan, 2026-10-25, from the read-only planning pass over lanes 1 and 1b (both done, uncommitted). Decisions below are binding for lane E1; evidence was read from source and primary sources opened that day. Unopened sources are marked.

## Findings in the shipped code that shape the work
- F3/F4: home unrest is 78% city, 17% region, 5% nation scope. `unrestReaching` counts nation-scope unrest for every state, so §253 opens in every opposed-governor state at once. §251 or §253 is always open at severity >= 0.6; the worry that §253 is rarely reachable is backwards.
- F5 bug: an immigration operation has no `MISSION_ATTENTION` row, so `missionWeeks` returns 0; it never shows in `liveLocaleMissions` and never supplies the mission an `injunction-enforcement` application needs.
- F6 bug: `courtApplicationFlag(kind, state, outcome)` stores the drawn outcome at filing; raw `player.flags` readers see the answer before `decideTurn`.
- F7: player-core prices options off the packet, which already carries the seeded suit (standing -0.03, stability -0.02) and incident; and `answers` reads `"with-assent"` as true even when the governor refuses.
- F8: funding lapse blocks the commitment as well as the act (`openCommitment` passes no excepted flag; it tests a full year).
- F9: `request-deployment` costs about $2.75B against about $105M for troops, which biases any dominance result.

## Decisions for lane E1 (engine source, one writer)
Files: `locale-missions.ts`, `locale-acts.ts`, `locale-act-effects.ts`, `commitments.ts`, `court-orders.ts`, `court-order-records.ts`, `mission-interim-relief.ts`, `force-costs.ts`; world `force-cost-anchors.ts` and `seed/force-cost-anchors.tsv`.

1. Stacked missions (Trump v. Illinois, 25A443; 10 U.S.C. §§252-253 call the militia themselves): replace federalize's `"proclamation"` ground with `"regular-forces"` (§12406(3)): open only with a live proclamation in the state AND an operative troops commitment there signed at least `REGULAR_FORCES_WEEKS = 2` earlier AND unrest >= `INSURRECTION_SEVERITY.obstruction` (0.5). `invasion` and `rebellion` unchanged. Cap: one live federalize and one live troops mission per state; federalize not in the same week as troops; refusal "a mission already in force here". Attention stays `Math.max` (stacking adds cost and risk, not attention). New federalize refusal: "an invasion, a rebellion, or an Insurrection Act mission here that the regular forces have not been able to carry out: 10 U.S.C. §12406(3), which Trump v. Illinois (2025) reads as reaching only where the military could legally execute the laws and could not do it. A declaration or a protest is none of them". Accepted: the troops act stands in for the Insurrection Act's own militia call.
2. Reachability: fix F5 with a `MISSION_WEEKS` table giving immigration-operation 8 weeks, separate from attention. Default remedy: require placed unrest (`condition.state === s`) for §252 and §253; nation-scope unrest opens only §251. Conditional remedies (apply only if the census shows the defect): injunction floor in `approvalAndNarrowing` 0.2 -> 0.3 if the court path is dead; `REBELLION_INSURGENCY_FLOOR` 0.2 -> 0.15 if rebellion is dead in the worst-case scenario.
3. Planning values. Rows for `FORCE_COST_ANCHORS` (same in the tsv; `seed-tables.test.ts` holds them together), read by new `domesticGuardWeek()` and `domesticBrigadeWeek()` in `force-costs.ts` which the federalize and troops `commitment.amountPerTurn` call instead of literals:
   - `guard-domestic-thousand-month` 19.5 usd_millions, 2026 dollars, sourced: CBO letter to Sen. Merkley (2026-01-28), $18M to $21M per 1,000 members a month. The CBO PDF returned 403; figures read from OPB/NPR https://www.opb.org/article/2026/01/28/cbo-projects-national-guard-deployments-could-cost-over-dollar1-billion-this-year/ and CBS https://www.cbsnews.com/news/troop-deployments-to-u-s-cities-cost-nearly-half-a-billion-dollars-in-2025/.
   - `guard-call-up` 3000 personnel, planning value (between Los Angeles's 4,000 and Chicago's few hundred, near the 2,950 in Washington).
   - `active-border-deployment-cost` 132 usd_millions, 2018 dollars, sourced: Joint Staff figure for active-duty troops on the border from early November 2018 to 2019-01-29, https://www.military.com/daily-news/2019/01/29/cost-deploying-active-duty-troops-border-132-million-and-counting.html (about 5,900 falling to about 2,100).
   - `active-border-deployment-weeks` 12 weeks, planning value.
   Results: federalize about $13.5M a week (3,000 x 19.5 x 7/30.44); troops about $14.5M a week ($132M / 12 x 331.655/251.107). Cross-check: LA June 2025, $134M for 60 days (Military Times), about $15.6M a week. Up-front $20M and $25M stay labeled planning values. Officer cost stays about $300k loaded, cited to FBI average $305k per position (Wray FY2025 testimony) and ICE ERO $208.08k pay and benefits per FTE (ICE FY2026 budget justification); no table row. Add the sources section "Domestic deployments (opened 2026-10-25)" and a "what is thin" note to `packages/world/sources/force-costs-sources.md`: CBO original not opened, border figure has no end date, LA figure is O&M only. Unverified: LA June 7 order size, Portland/Chicago head counts, brigade size.
4. F9: reprice `request-deployment` to 3,000 Guard for 8 weeks on the same anchor: about $108M, `treasuryOfGdp: -3.9e-6`. The relief entry's literal `    treasuryOfGdp: -0.00025,` stays byte-identical.
5. Priority memoranda keyed by state: pass `key: id` to `issueDirective` in `priorityDirective`; `priorityRefused` checks `directive:INST-DOJ:enforcement-posture:${turn}:${id}`; court applications use their own key prefix so they cannot collide. Test: two states in one week both carried; same state twice refused; a court application the same week does not block it.
6. Two-week minimum as a billing floor, not a refusal: when an `authorizedBy` commitment lapses because its order was rescinded and fewer than `MISSION_MINIMUM_WEEKS = 2` weeks were served, charge the remaining weeks x `amountPerTurn`. A court suspension or strike-down carries no floor. `setCommitmentStatus` refuses a hand wind-down of an `authorizedBy` commitment ("Rescind the order that sent them").
7. Kept-promises standing keeps excluding state-targeted commitments (including them would refund most of §251's standing cost); add a test.
8. Funding lapse: 31 U.S.C. §1342 excepts "emergencies involving the safety of human life or the protection of property". Mark federalize, troops, task-force and immigration-operation `excepted: true`; add `CommitmentDraft.excepted?: true` passed by `missionCommitment` and honored in `moneyShortfall`. Accepted: `moneyShortfall` tests a year, not the term.
9. Early-read flag (N1): at filing write `court-application:<kind>:<state>` with the turn marker plus the shown odds as integer percents (`:g<pct>:n<pct>`); at `decideTurn` the ruling phase draws `stream("court-order", kind, state, appliedTurn)` against the stored odds and writes `courtRulingFlag`; `courtOrderRecords` reads the outcome only from the ruling flag. Seed-stable, no state widening. Tests: no flag carries granted/narrowed/denied before `decideTurn`; outcome distribution over 40 seeds unchanged within +-0.02.
10. TRO breadth (N3): `STATE_PLAINTIFF_RELIEF` applies only when the governor did not assent; `INTERIM_HARM` task-force 0.3 -> 0.1. Bands when contested, split by act: federalize and §252/§253 troops 35-75% enjoined; property-surge and task-force <= 15%; §251 troops <= 10%. An enjoined proclamation does not recall troops already sent (accepted).

## Accepted risks (with reason)
Invasion read off any war the US defends (no territory on Conflict); the troops act stands in for the Insurrection Act's militia call; CBO figures are press-reported; court odds and the injunction term other than the FISA cap are planning values; `moneyShortfall` tests a year not the term; an enjoined proclamation does not recall troops already sent; a narrowed injunction counts for §252.
