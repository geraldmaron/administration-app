## Binding and follow-through

The earlier statement that report binding does not exist is stale. `ScenarioCast` now has `collectionReport` with target, recipient, and home bindings. `ScenarioCondition` gates target kind, status, topic, basis, condition, confidence, exposure, sharing, recipient effect, and active tasking. `scenario-report.ts` selects and freezes an issued report; `scenario-cast.ts` resolves its counterpart and tokens and pins `occurrence` to the report ID; `scenario-cases.ts` snapshots it in the open case. `scenario-audit-report.ts` rejects ungrounded or classified claims and conflicting authored costs for a direct state response. These contracts are implemented and tested, but **zero of 339 mounted definitions uses them**. A small report-bound pilot can now be authored without another report binder; live exposure and independent review still need proof.

Other typed gates exist but are unused by the mounted pool: `stateGrievance`, `cohortStrain`, `oversightMatter`, and `pendingRule`. The housing bill has substantive base/targeted texts and a conference route, while authored conditions cannot bind bill ID/text/stage or enacted law. Court procedure tracks threshold, interim, merits, appeal, and final history, while authored conditions cannot bind case identity, holding, remedy, or party. Writing a particular housing-text or case-stage sequel today would invent its connection. Those two binders remain prerequisites.
