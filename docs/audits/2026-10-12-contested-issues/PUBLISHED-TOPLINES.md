Researched 2026-10-12. Purpose: (A) rake targets for the religion axis by race and ethnicity, and (B) outside figures for issues the CES covers thinly (LGBTQ, schools, religious exemptions, death penalty, marijuana) plus an abortion cross-check of the CES numbers. Accuracy standard per the owner: reasonable, not exact. Whole percents, one pass, no hunting. Where a figure is my arithmetic on published numbers it says "derived". Where a page would not open and I relied on search-result text, it says "snippet".

## Mapping notes

1. White evangelical Protestant: PRRI "white evangelical Protestant" exactly (born-again or evangelical self-ID, White non-Hispanic). Pew's "evangelical Protestant" (denominational, all races, 70% White) is a looser match; Pew prayer, death penalty and marijuana lines use "White evangelical Protestant" as published.
2. White mainline Protestant: PRRI "white mainline/non-evangelical Protestant". Pew P6 and P7 use "White mainline" or "White non-evangelical Protestant"; P8 uses "White Protestant, not evangelical". All three treat non-born-again White Protestants alike, matching our CES rule.
3. Black Protestant: PRRI "Black Protestant" (race plus Protestant). Pew P6, P7 and P8 say "Black Protestant", which Pew defines by race here, not by historically Black denomination. Pew's RLS SSM figure (56) is the denominational "historically Black Protestant" line.
4. Catholic: all races, derived from PRRI white, Hispanic and other-of-color Catholic lines. Pew P6, P7, P8 give "Catholic" overall.
5. Other Christian: PRRI Hispanic Protestant, other Protestant of color, LDS, Orthodox and JW lines, weighted by national share. Orthodox and JW added where published. This is a derived mix, not a measured group, and Hispanic Protestants (a mostly evangelical group) dominate it.
6. Other faiths: PRRI Jewish, Muslim, Buddhist, Hindu lines (and UU where listed), weighted. Muslims diverge sharply from the other three on LGBTQ items, so the mix hides a split.
7. Unaffiliated: PRRI "religiously unaffiliated" (atheist, agnostic, nothing in particular) and Pew "religiously unaffiliated". Same definition.
8. PRRI survey years differ by item: LGBTQ and abortion (AVA 2023, 22,000+ interviews, online), transition-care item (AVA 2024). Pew items differ: 2021, 2026. State the year when quoting.
9. Rounding: PRRI publishes whole percents; "derived" averages are rounded again. Expect plus or minus 2 on derived cells, plus or minus 4 on the White row in Part A.

## Gaps

- Pew's White, Black (tradition detail) and Asian race pages would not open (403). White and Asian composition are derived, and there is no published evangelical/mainline split for Black or Asian adults. Asian "Christian 34, none 32" is from a search result summarizing the RLS and was not opened.
- A direct race-by-tradition table from PRRI was not looked for beyond the national Census report.
- Transgender athletes by tradition, public money for religious schools by tradition, and a clean religious-exemptions support share: not found. Transition-care by tradition is given as opposition to restrictions only.
- Death penalty, marijuana and school prayer: nothing found for other Christian or other faiths. Death penalty and marijuana data are from 2021; newer Pew or Gallup tradition cuts were not located. No GSS summaries were opened.
- The LDS nondiscrimination figure and the Hindu same-sex marriage figure could not be read reliably from the PRRI chart text; the narrative text and chart disagreed by up to 2 points on a few nondiscrimination cells (for example Jewish 81 in text versus 83 in the chart), and I used the narrative.
- Pew RLS 2025 abortion and same-sex marriage figures by tradition came only as search snippets because the report page returned 403.
